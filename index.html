About Mira 💗

Mira is an AI companion demo designed to chat in a sweet, shy and supportive Hinglish style.

Current version: replies use simple local rules. This is not a real person and is not yet connected to a generative AI model.

Voice recognition and spoken replies depend on browser support. Microphone recognition may be processed by your browser's service.
